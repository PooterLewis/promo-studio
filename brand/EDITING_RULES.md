# Kyrn promo editing rules

Every Kyrn short follows these rules unless Jack says otherwise for that edit.
Text comes from `kyrn_text.py` (fonts in `fonts/`, all SIL Open Font License).

## Text style
Built on Kyrn's own UI style sheet (Kyrn repo `art/ui/style/sheet.png`) and the Prism skin palette.
- **Flat color only.** No gradients, no glow, no blur, no colored outlines. Depth comes only from hard edges: a thin dark rim and a hard dark step down and right.
- **The letters are the alien part:** every letter is cut like the game's icons, with a lit face (top left) and a shaded face meeting at a hard diagonal, like cut crystal or carved shell. In the prism look, titles get a thin aurora seam (teal, violet, green) along each cut.
- Titles use wide capitals (Krona One, close to the game's Builder Extended) with a **Vael script line** underneath, like every title in the game.
- Captions use Krona One in capitals with no seams, so they stay readable.
- The call to action sits on the game's six-sided crystal-cut button: dark text on a solid plate, never colored text floating on footage.
- Looks: **shell** (the game UI: bone `#efe7d6`/`#c2b496`, vein `#5fb5a7`, gold `#d4a24c`, abyss `#0f1c22`) and **prism** (glass `#f2f6fb`/`#b0c0d6`, aurora `#5fe0cc` `#a78bff` `#7cf0a0`, slate `#454a72`). **House look (Jack, Oct 20): the mix. The KYRN logo is prism (`title`), captions and the button are shell.** The Halloween look comes later from the game's hollow palette (ember `#ff8c32`, violet).
- Three roles only: `title` (the logo, end card only), `caption` (gameplay text), `cta` (call to action). All caps, at most 2 lines, about 5 words per line.

## Captions
- Keep captions to the four simple beats, in order (Jack, Oct 20: "you're getting too detailed"): **BUILD YOUR CITY**, **TRAIN YOUR TROOPS**, **SET UP AN INCOME**, **SEND THEM TO BATTLE**. Each beat spans a few shots that show it. No stats, unit names or mechanics.
- Show plenty of close-up fighting, not only zoomed-out base shots.
- A caption must match what is on screen while it is up (Jack, Oct 20): BUILD shots show a building actually going up (build menu open, construction beams), never a training panel or a failed placement.
- Open with KYRN plus "NEW ALIEN RTS GAME / COMING TO ROBLOX" over a lightly blurred fight for about 3 seconds, then go into the game.
- Plain and factual, never a slogan. Jack called "62 UNITS. ONE ORDER." corny. Good: "A NEW RTS GAME", or a unit or building name.
- Banned: trailer clichés such as "unleash chaos", "no mercy", "crush your enemies", "raise your army".
- A caption stays up for its whole shot, at least 1.5 seconds.

## Branding
- "KYRN" appears once, on the end card. No repeated tag on every shot.
- CTA wording: **COMING SOON TO ROBLOX**, on the gold button on the end card (optionally also over the last gameplay shot).

## Story and clip choice
- 15 to 30 seconds, 6 to 10 shots, each 1.5 to 4 seconds.
- Open on the single best moment in the first 1.5 seconds.
- Show at least 3 different kinds of moment (building, army selection, different unit types, monsters, a fight, a map view). Never more than 2 shots in a row of the same fight.
- If one recording doesn't have enough variety, use other takes and say so.

## Framing (1080x1920)
- Keep everything inside the TikTok safe area (Jack, Oct 20): top 210px (search bar), right 140px (like/comment buttons) and bottom 440px (caption) stay clear. Gameplay is 880x990 at x=60, y=400 with soft feathered edges; text is centered on x=500 (the middle of the safe area), at most 840px wide, captions at y=330.
- Blurred, darkened gameplay fills the rest. No empty band at the top: the caption sits over the top seam.
- Keep text out of the bottom 380px and top 150px (TikTok/Reels buttons).

## Sound
- Real battle sound under every fight (Jack, Oct 20), with the music ducked under it. Only takes recorded with a separate "game sound" track have audio; pick fight clips from those. Battle sound plays only under battle shots; build, train and income shots get music only. Level each clip with a fixed gain (never per-clip loudnorm), let the music duck smoothly under the fights, and level the whole video once at the end with two-pass loudnorm.
- Background music (the game's Waking theme) under the whole edit, cut to the beat where possible.
- Music must be Jack's own Kyrn soundtrack or royalty-free and safe for TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.
- Game sound is very quiet when recorded, so normalize the final mix to -14 LUFS, true peak -1.5 dB.

## Delivery
- Save as `Claude - <short title>.mp4` at the top of the Promo Studio folder, H.264 + AAC, 1080x1920, 30 or 60 fps.
- Before delivering, check a contact sheet of every shot against these rules.

Getting Discord and game sound
  • Windows: choose “Entire screen” and tick Share system audio. That records the game and your friends on Discord.
  • Sharing a single window records video only. Chrome can't capture one app's sound.
  • Mac: recent Chrome on macOS 14.2+ offers system audio for a whole screen. If it doesn't, use a loopback tool such as BlackHole and pick it as the mic.
  • Wear headphones so your mic doesn't pick up Discord a second time. Your voice and the game are saved as separate tracks, so you can balance them in the editor.
  • This tab can sit in the background while you play. Recording keeps going.
